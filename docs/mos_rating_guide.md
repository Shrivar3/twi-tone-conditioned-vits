# Twi TTS - Listening Evaluation (MOS) Guide

Thank you for helping us evaluate our Twi text-to-speech voices!
This should take about 20–30 minutes.

## What you'll be doing
You'll listen to short Twi audio clips (each a few seconds long) and rate each one on **two things**. You won't know which system produced each clip — that's on purpose, so your ratings stay fair.

## How to rate each clip
For every clip, give **two separate scores from 1 to 5**:

**1. Naturalness** — Does it sound like a real human speaking Twi?
- 5 = completely natural, sounds human
- 4 = mostly natural
- 3 = okay, somewhat robotic
- 2 = quite robotic/unnatural
- 1 = very robotic, not natural at all

**2. Intelligibility** — How clearly can you understand the words?
- 5 = every word is perfectly clear
- 4 = mostly clear
- 3 = understandable with some effort
- 2 = hard to understand
- 1 = cannot understand the words

## How to fill it in
Open the rating sheet (`mos_rating_sheet.csv`) in Excel or Google Sheets. For each row:
1. Play the audio file named in the `audio_file` column.
2. Put your score (1–5) in the `naturalness` column.
3. Put your score (1–5) in the `intelligibility` column.
4. (Optional) Add a short note in `comments` if something stood out.

## A few tips
- Listen to each clip once or twice — go with your honest first impression.
- Rate based on how it *sounds*, not whether you agree with the sentence content.
- There are no right or wrong answers — your native judgement is exactly what we need.
- Please try to rate all the clips, but a partial sheet is still useful.

When you're done, just send the filled-in sheet back to us. Thank you so much! 🙏
